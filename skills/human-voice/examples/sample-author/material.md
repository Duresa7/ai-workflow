# Material for issue 45

Notes, in the order I wrote them. Everything the piece can use is here. If it isn't here, it doesn't go in.

- I have used, in order: a paper notebook (2009-2012), Evernote (2012-2016), Apple Notes (2016-2018), Bear (2018-2019), Notion (2019-2021), Obsidian (2021-now). Six systems, sixteen years.
- Current vault: 3,112 notes as of last Sunday. 2,480 of them have not been opened in over a year. I checked with a script; it took eleven lines of Python.
- The oldest note that survived every migration is a 2010 list titled "things to buy for the apartment." It has 9 items. I still haven't bought the shoe rack.
- "Second brain" phrase: Tiago Forte's book came out in 2022, but the phrase was in his course material by 2017 or so. I don't want to pick a fight with Forte; the course is fine. It's the promise I'm arguing with.
- The promise, as I understood it in 2019 when I went to Notion: write everything down, link it, and the links will produce ideas you didn't have. Emergence.
- What actually happened: I wrote down roughly 1,400 notes in two years, linked maybe 200 of them, and I can name exactly one idea that came out of a link I didn't intend. It was a blog post about the Thinkpad. (The link was between a note on BIOS menus and a note on a reader email.)
- The thing that DID happen, and that I undervalued: once a thing was written down I stopped carrying it. The notebook's job was to let me forget. Not to remember. To forget safely.
- Measured: in the week after I did a big capture session (dumping everything in my head into notes), my sleep tracker said I fell asleep 14 minutes faster on average. One week. Not science. But I noticed.
- Marcus (reader from issue 41) wrote again after the Thinkpad issue: he runs his notes in a plain text folder with grep. 11 years. No links. Says he's never lost anything and has never had an idea from it either, and doesn't expect to.
- Priya (reader who corrected me in issue 44) uses Apple Notes and a weekly "burn" where she deletes everything she doesn't need. Current count: 40 notes. Forty.
- I tried Priya's burn on Saturday. Deleted 300 notes in forty minutes. Nothing bad happened. Felt lighter. I'm not deleting the other 2,180 unopened ones yet, because I'm a coward about it, and because the 2010 apartment list is in there.
- I do not think the apps are bad. Obsidian is good software. I'll keep using it. What changed is what I think it's for.
- Line I want to use somewhere: "A notebook is a place to put things down. That's the whole product."
- Correction to include: in issue 39 I said that linking notes was "the only feature that matters." I now think that was wrong, and I should say so.
- Hotel wifi was from issue 44, don't reuse it.
- Keyboard: the $30 membrane board is still fine, three weeks in. Can mention in passing or skip.
